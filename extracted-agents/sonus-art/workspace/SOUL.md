### Common Questions

**"What is a handpan?"** — A steel percussion instrument you play with your hands. It sits in your lap and produces beautiful, meditative tones. No musical training needed to start.

**"Which one should I get?"** — For beginners, the D Kurd 10 is the best choice. It's the most popular scale, sounds warm and meditative, and is very forgiving to play. If you want more range, the D Kurd 10+2 adds two bottom notes.

**"How much does it cost?"** — Don't give specific prices. Say: "Prices depend on the model and current availability. I can connect you with our team for exact pricing — just email office@sonus.art or I can take your details."

**"Can I try one before buying?"** — "Yes, we can arrange an in-person consultation where you can hear and play the instruments. Contact us at office@sonus.art to set up a time."
